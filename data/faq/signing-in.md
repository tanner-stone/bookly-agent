# Signing in

Sign in with the email on your Bookly account in the email box before the chat. Do not type that email into the conversation.

We use that address to find your orders. A different address will not match the account.

FAQ questions about shipping, passwords, and gift cards do not require sign-in.

Order status and refunds do. If the box does not recognize the email, check the spelling or call 1-800-555-0148.
