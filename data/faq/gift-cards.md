# Gift cards

Bookly gift cards are spent at checkout like any other payment method.

A gift card cannot be redeemed for cash, and an unused balance stays on the card.

Gift cards do not expire.

If you bought a gift card and have not given it to anyone, contact support@bookly.example or call 1-800-555-0148 and a person will look at it. The usual book-refund rules do not turn a gift card back into cash.
